How it works

Step 1

Clean the data

Invoice numbers lose prefixes, punctuation, spaces and leading zeros, so INV-001023 and 1023 become the same. Vendor names drop case, punctuation and suffixes like Inc or LLC.

Step 2

Score similarity

Invoices are grouped by amount, then compared within each group. Edit distance counts the character changes between two invoice numbers and turns it into a 0–100% score.

Step 3

Flag with a reason

High: numbers match after cleaning. Medium: numbers are similar enough to be a typo. Low: worth a look, such as different numbers a few days apart, or sequential numbers a month apart that are probably recurring bills.
